Before the GENIUS Act, a USD 10 bn stablecoin inflow was associated with 3-month T-bill yields 10.9 bp lower ten business days later. After the Act: 5.6 bp. I had pre-registered the opposite.

The Act (July 2025) limits issuers' Treasury reserves to maturities of 93 days or less; it binds from January 2027.

A pre-registered replication-in-spirit of Rashad Ahmed and Iñaki Aldasoro's "Stablecoins and safe asset prices" (BIS WP 1270), extended 15 months past enactment. Three hypotheses, three nulls:

1) Stronger response after the Act: −10.9 bp before (90% CI −17.7 to −4.1) vs −5.6 bp after (−13.0 to +1.9); difference +5.3 bp, p = 0.30.
2) Shift toward ≤ 93-day maturities: the short-end tilt (4-week −14.8 bp, 2-year ≈ 0) predates the Act.
3) Outflows dominate: +16.9 vs −1.6 bp per USD 3.5 bn before the Act; after it, −3.7 vs −4.9. 321 of 412 pre-Act outflow days fall in the 2022–23 hiking cycle.

The result I most want challenged: a placebo break in July 2024, with no legislation, shifts the estimate by +23.1 bp (p < 0.01), over four times the GENIUS shift. Controls-only estimates of this channel are unstable over time, an argument for the instrument in WP 1270.

Limits: associations, not causal effects; short post-Act sample (detectable change ≈ 15 bp); rules not yet binding.
